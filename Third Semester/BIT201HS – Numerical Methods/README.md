# BIT201HS – Numerical Methods

**Year II | Semester I (Third Semester) | Credits: 3**

## Teaching and Examination
Theory: 3 hrs/week | Tutorial: 1 hr/week | Practical: 2 hrs/week  
Internal: Theory 20, Practical 50 | Final: Theory 80 | Total: 150

## Course Objective
Solve nonlinear equations, use interpolation, fit curves, solve linear equations, and perform numerical integration and differentiation using computers.

## Course Contents

### 1. Errors in Numerical Computation — 3 Hrs
- Introduction to numerical method
- Introduction to error
- Sources of error
- General errors formula

### 2. Solution of Nonlinear Equations — 6 Hrs
- Introduction; Bisection method; Newton-Raphson method; Secant method; Horner's rule

### 3. Interpolation — 10 Hrs
- Introduction and finite differences: forward, backward, central differences, symbolic relations
- Newton's Forward and Backward formulae
- Lagrange interpolation
- Least Square methods: linear (y = a + bx), quadratic (y = a + bx + cx²), y = ax^b, and y = ae^(bx)

### 4. System of Linear Equations — 11 Hrs
- Contingency of a linear system of equations
- Direct methods: Gaussian Elimination, Gauss-Jordan, Matrix inversion
- Indirect methods: Gauss-Jacobi and Gauss-Seidel iteration
- LU Decomposition
- Eigen vectors and Eigen values; Power method

### 5. Numerical Differentiation and Integration — 8 Hrs
- First and second order numerical differentiation: forward and backward formulae
- Trapezoidal rule; Simpson's 1/3 and 3/8 rules; Romberg integration

### 6. Numerical Solution of Ordinary Differential Equations — 7 Hrs
- Introduction
- Euler's and Modified Euler's methods
- Runge-Kutta 2nd and 4th order methods
- Boundary value problem (Finite Difference method)
