# BIT206CO – Project-III

**Year II | Semester I (Third Semester) | Credits: 2**

## Teaching and Examination
Theory: – | Tutorial: – | Practical: 3 hrs/week  
Internal Practical: 60 | Final Practical: 40 | Total: 100

## Course Objective
After finishing this project, students will be able to develop a microcontroller-based project.

## Course Contents
A project group will develop a microcontroller (BIT202CO)-based system. Every student in the group should work at least **45 lab hours** under the supervision of the assigned supervisor. Students must develop the assigned software, submit a written report, and give an oral presentation.

## Project Evaluation Criteria for Internal Assessment

| Evaluation Component | Marks |
|---|---:|
| Title identification and Proposal Writing | 10 |
| Mid-term Presentation | 20 |
| Pre-final Submission and final Presentation | 30 |
| **Total Internal Practical** | **60** |

**Group Size:** 2 to 3 students in a group.

## Project Deliverables
- Microcontroller-based system
- Assigned software
- Written report
- Oral presentation

Final practical examination: **40 marks**.
