# BIT203CO – Data Structure and Algorithm

**Year II | Semester I (Third Semester) | Credits: 3**

## Teaching and Examination
Theory: 3 hrs/week | Tutorial: 1 hr/week | Practical: 2 hrs/week  
Internal: Theory 20, Practical 50 | Final: Theory 80 | Total: 150

## Course Objective
Provide fundamental knowledge of data structure, various algorithms, and their implementations.

## Course Contents

1. **Introduction — 2 Hrs:** Data and data types; Data Structure (DS), Abstract Data Type (ADT) and applications; ADT vs Data Structure.
2. **Algorithm Efficiency and Complexity — 2 Hrs:** Time and Space Analysis; asymptotic notations: big O, sigma, theta, omega.
3. **Stack — 5 Hrs:** Definition and examples; Stack as ADT; array representation; primitive operations and efficiency; overflow/underflow; prefix, infix and postfix expressions; evaluation and conversion using stack.
4. **Queue — 4 Hrs:** Definition and examples; Queue as ADT; array representation; primitive operations and efficiency; overflow/underflow; linear and circular queue; priority queue.
5. **List and Linked List — 6 Hrs:** List as ADT and primary operations; static and dynamic list; linked list as ADT; singly/doubly linear and circular linked lists; advantages of doubly over singly linked list; insertion/deletion at front, last, before/after a given node; dummy node; linked-list implementation of stack and queue.
6. **Recursion — 3 Hrs:** Principle; recursion vs iteration with advantages/disadvantages; Fibonacci sequence, TOH and multiplication of natural numbers with algorithm efficiency.
7. **Trees — 6 Hrs:** Concepts, definitions and properties; binary tree and linked-list representation; BST; insertion/deletion; preorder, inorder and postorder traversal; height, depth and level; AVL tree and balancing; Huffman algorithm.
8. **Sorting — 6 Hrs:** Internal and external sorting; insertion, selection and bubble sort; quick sort and merge sort; radix sort; shell sort; heap sort as priority queue; efficiency.
9. **Searching and Hashing — 4 Hrs:** Searching concepts and keys; sequential search, binary search, BST; general search tree; hash functions and hash table; collision resolution; efficiency of searching methods.
10. **Graphs — 7 Hrs:** Definition, representation, applications and ADT; adjacency matrix and transitive closure; Warshall's algorithm; graph types; DFS and BFS; spanning tree/forest; minimum spanning tree and greedy approach (Kruskal's, Prim's and Round Robin algorithms); Dijkstra's shortest-path algorithm.
