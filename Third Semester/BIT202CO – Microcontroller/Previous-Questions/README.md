## Group A
**Answer TWO questions.** *(2 × 12 = 24)*

1. What do you mean by serial communication & parallel communication? Explain RS-232 standard of communication & null MODEM implementation.

2. Draw and explain the pin diagram of 8051 microcontroller.

3. Write an assembly language program to display your first name in a LCD module along with necessary circuit.
