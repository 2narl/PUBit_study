# BIT155MS – Financial Management and Accounting

**Program:** Bachelor in Information Technology (BIT)  
**University:** Purbanchal University (PU)  
**Year:** I  
**Semester:** II

This course introduces the fundamentals of financial management and accounting, including financial statements, accounting processes, and financial decision-making. Refer to the official PU syllabus for the complete unit-wise contents and teaching hours.

## Course Contents

The following topics provide a brief study overview and are not a substitute for the official syllabus:

1. Introduction to Financial Management
2. Financial Objectives and Decision-Making
3. Financial Statements
4. Accounting Principles and Concepts
5. Journal, Ledger and Trial Balance
6. Final Accounts and Financial Statements
7. Cash Flow Statement
8. Working Capital Management
9. Time Value of Money and Investment Decisions

> **Note:** Always follow the latest official Purbanchal University syllabus for exact unit titles, topic coverage, teaching hours, and examination structure.
