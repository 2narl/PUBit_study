# BIT152CO – Digital Logic

**Program:** Bachelor in Information Technology (BIT)  
**University:** Purbanchal University (PU)  
**Year:** I  
**Semester:** II

## Course Overview

Digital Logic introduces number systems, Boolean algebra, logic gates, and the digital circuits used to design and understand computer hardware.

## Main Topics

- Number systems and codes
- Boolean algebra and logic gates
- Karnaugh maps (K-maps)
- Combinational logic circuits
- Adders, subtractors, and comparators
- Encoders, decoders, multiplexers, and demultiplexers
- Sequential logic circuits
- Registers, counters, and flip-flops
- Programmable logic devices (PLDs), including PLA and ROM

## How to Prepare

1. Understand number systems, Boolean algebra, and logic gates before moving to circuit design.
2. Practise K-map simplification and Boolean expression problems.
3. Draw and practise the truth tables and logic diagrams of combinational circuits.
4. Study registers, counters, and flip-flops with their timing diagrams.
5. Practise previous-year questions after completing the relevant topics.
