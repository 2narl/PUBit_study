# BIT151HS – Mathematics-II

**Program:** Bachelor in Information Technology (BIT)  
**University:** Purbanchal University (PU)  
**Year:** I  
**Semester:** II

## Course Overview

Mathematics-II develops mathematical techniques used in computing and engineering, including differential equations, Fourier analysis, and complex-variable methods.

## Main Topics

- Differential equations (first-order and higher-order)
- Fourier series and Fourier integrals
- Complex numbers and complex functions
- Analytic functions and Cauchy–Riemann equations
- Complex integration and Cauchy's theorems
- Taylor and Laurent series
- Singularities, poles, and residues
- Contour integration
