# BIT151HS – Mathematics-II

**Program:** Bachelor in Information Technology (BIT)  
**University:** Purbanchal University (PU)  
**Year:** I  
**Semester:** II

This course develops mathematical methods used in computing, including differential equations, complex variables, and related mathematical techniques. Refer to the official PU syllabus for the complete unit-wise contents and teaching hours.

## Course Contents

The following topics provide a brief study overview and are not a substitute for the official syllabus:

1. Differential Equations
2. Higher-Order Linear Differential Equations
3. Fourier Series and Fourier Integrals
4. Complex Variables and Functions
5. Analytic Functions and Cauchy–Riemann Equations
6. Complex Integration and Cauchy's Theorems
7. Taylor and Laurent Series
8. Singularities, Poles and Residues
9. Contour Integration

> **Note:** Always follow the latest official Purbanchal University syllabus for exact unit titles, topic coverage, teaching hours, and examination structure.
