## Course Overview

Mathematics-II develops mathematical techniques used in computing and engineering. It covers differential equations, Fourier analysis, and complex-variable methods.

## Main Topics

- Multiple integration
- Ordinary and partial differential equations
- Fourier series, Fourier integrals, and Fourier transforms
- Complex numbers and complex functions
- Analytic functions and Cauchy–Riemann equations
- Complex integration and Cauchy's theorems
- Taylor and Laurent series
- Singularities, poles, and residues
- Contour integration
