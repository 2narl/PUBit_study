**Bachelor in Information Technology (B.I.T.) / Second Semester / Final**  
**BIT151HS: Mathematics-II (New Course)**

*Candidates are required to give their answers in their own words as far as practicable. Figures in the margin indicate full marks.*

---

## Group A

**Answer ALL questions. [10 × 2 = 20]**

1. Evaluate: $\int_{0}^{a}\int_{0}^{a}\int_{0}^{a}(x+y+z)\ dx\ dy\ dz$.

2. Evaluate the integral by changing the order of integration: $\int_{0}^{\infty}\int_{y}^{\infty}\frac{e^{-y}}{y}\,dy\,dx$.


3. Determine the order and degree of the differential equation: $\sqrt{\frac{d^3y}{dx^3}}=\frac{dy}{dx}$.

4. Solve: $(x+1)\,dy=(y-1)\,dx$.

5. Solve: $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-4\frac{dy}{dx}+13y=0$.

6. Determine whether the function $f(x)=\log\left|\frac{1-x}{1+x}\right|$ is even or odd.

7. Express the function $f(z)=\sin z$ in the form $u(x,y)+iv(x,y)$.

8. If $f(z)$ is differentiable at $z_0$, show that $f(z)$ is continuous at $z=z_0$.

9. Find the zeros of the function $f(z)=\left(\frac{z+2}{z^2+1}\right)^3$.

10. Show that the function $f(z)=\frac{z^2-2z+5}{z-2}$ has a simple pole at $z=2$.

---

## Group B

**Answer EIGHT questions. [8 × 5 = 40]**

11. Evaluate: $\iint_R xy\,dx\,dy$, where $R$ is the positive quadrant of the circle $x^2+y^2=a^2$.

12. Solve: $\frac{dy}{dx}+\frac{y}{x^2}=\frac{1}{x^2}$.

13. Find the general solution of the differential equation: $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-\frac{dy}{dx}-2y=6e^x$.

14. Find the Fourier series for $f(x)=2x$, $0\leq x\leq\pi$, where $f(x)=f(x+2\pi)$.

15. Expand the following function in Fourier cosine series: $f(x)=1$ for $0<x<\frac{\pi}{2}$, and $f(x)=0$ for $\frac{\pi}{2}<x<\pi$.

16. Verify the Cauchy–Riemann equations for the function $f(z)=\frac{x-iy}{x^2+y^2}$.

17. Expand $f(z)=\frac{1}{z}$ by Taylor's series about the point $z=1$.

18. Solve: $x\frac{dy}{dx}=y+x^2\log x$.

19. State and prove the Cauchy–Riemann equations.

20. A function $f(x)$ is defined by $f(x)=1$ for $-1\leq x\leq 1$, and $f(x)=0$ otherwise. Find the Fourier integral representation of $f(x)$.

---

## Group C

**Answer FOUR questions. [4 × 5 = 20]**

21. Solve: $\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{2x-y+1}{6x-5y+4}$.

22. Find the general solution of the differential equation: $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+2\frac{dy}{dx}+y=2x+x^2$.

23. Find the Fourier sine series for $f(x)=x^2$ in the interval $0<x<3$.

24. Find an analytic function $f(z)$ whose real part is $u=e^x(x\cos y-y\sin y)$.

25. Find the residue of $f(z)=\frac{z}{(z-1)(z-2)^2}$ at its poles.

## Group A

**Answer ALL questions. [10 × 2 = 20]**

1. Evaluate the double integral: $\int_{1}^{3}\int_{2}^{5}(2x^2+3y^2)\,dx\,dy$.

2. Solve: $(x+y)\,dy+(y-x)\,dx=0$.

3. Verify Cauchy–Riemann equations for the function $\cosh x+i\sinh y\cos x$.

4. A function $f(x)$ is defined as $f(x)=1$ for $0<x<1$, and $f(x)=0$ for $x\geq 1$. Find the Fourier cosine integral of $f(x)$.

5. Solve: $p^2-y^2=0$.

6. Express the function $f(z)=e^z$ in the form $u(x,y)+iv(x,y)$.

7. Find the zeros and poles of the function $f(z)=\left(\frac{z+1}{z^2+1}\right)^2$.

8. Define odd and even functions with examples.

9. Show that the function $f(z)=\frac{\sinh(z-z_0)}{z-z_0}$ has a removable singularity at $z=z_0$.

10. Define Fourier cosine series and Fourier sine series.
---

## Group B

**Answer EIGHT questions. [8 × 5 = 40]**

11. Solve: $x\frac{dy}{dx}-y\,dx=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\,dx$.

12. Solve: $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+16y=\cos 4x$.

13. Express $f(x)=|x|$, $-\pi<x<\pi$, as a Fourier series.

14. Find the Fourier transform of $f(x)=1-x^2$ for $|x|\leq 1$, and $f(x)=0$ for $|x|>1$.

15. Expand $f(z)=\frac{1}{z^2-3z+2}$ in the regions:  
    (a) $|z|<1$  
    (b) $1<|z|<2$

16. Solve the differential equation: $(D^2-3D+2)y=e^{5x}$.

17. Solve: $\frac{dy}{dx}+y\tan x=\sec x$.

18. Evaluate $\int_{0}^{1}\int_{0}^{\sqrt{1-y^2}}e^{x^2}y\,dx\,dy$ by changing the order of integration.

19. Show that $u=e^x(x\cos y-y\sin y)$ is a harmonic function and find the analytic function $f(z)=u+iv$.

---

## Group C

**Answer FOUR questions. [4 × 5 = 20]**

20. The equations of motion of a particle are given by $\frac{dx}{dt}+wy-wx=0$ and $\frac{dy}{dt}-wx=0$. Find the path of the particle and show that it is a circle.

21. Solve the differential equation: $(D^2+4D+4)y=e^{-2x}+\cos 5x$.

22. Find the residue of $f(z)=\frac{3z-4}{(z-1)(z-2)}$ at each of its poles.

23. Find the Fourier transform of $f(x)=1$ for $|x|<1$, and $f(x)=0$ for $|x|>1$. Hence, evaluate $\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{\sin s}{s}\,ds$.

24. Find the Taylor expansion of $\frac{2z^3+1}{z^2+2}$ about the point $z=i$.

## Group B

**Answer EIGHT questions. (8 × 5 = 40)**

11. Define the Fourier integral of the function. Find the Fourier integral of the function $f(x)=x$ for $|x|<1$, and $f(x)=0$ for $|x|>1$.

12. Solve: $y''-2y'-4x=0$, with $y(0)=1$.

13. Find the general solution of the differential equation: $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-13\frac{dy}{dx}+12y=e^{-2x}$.

14. Find the Fourier series for the function $f(x)=x^2,\;0\le x\le\pi$.

15. Obtain the half-range cosine series for $f(x)=\sin x$ in $0\le x\le\pi$ and hence show that $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{4n^2-1}=\frac{1}{2}$.

16. Verify Cauchy-Riemann equation for the function $f(z)=e^x(\cos y+i\sin y)$.

17. Find the Laurent's series for $f(z)=\frac{1}{(1-z)(z+2)}$ valid for the domain $1<|z|<2$.

18. Solve: $x\frac{dy}{dx}+2y=x^2\log x$.

19. Find by double integration, the area which lies inside the cardioid $r=a(1+\cos\theta)$ and outside the circle $r=a$.

20. Solve: $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-2\frac{dy}{dx}+5y=10\sin x$.

## Group C

**Answer FOUR questions. (4 × 5 = 20)**

21. Solve: $\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{x+2y-3}{2x+y-3}$.

22. Find the general solution of the differential equation: $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-2\frac{dy}{dx}+4y=e^x\sin^2x$.

23. Obtain the Fourier series for $f(x)=x$ when $-1<x\le 0$, and $f(x)=x+2$ when $0<x\le 1$.

24. Find an analytic function $f(z)$ whose real part is $u(x,y)=e^x\sin y$.

25. Find the residue of $f(z)=\frac{3z-4}{z(z-1)(z-2)}$ at each of its poles.
