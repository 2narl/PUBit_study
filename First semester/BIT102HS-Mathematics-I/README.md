# BIT102HS – Mathematics-I

**Program:** Bachelor in Information Technology (BIT)  
**University:** Purbanchal University (PU)  
**Year:** I  
**Semester:** I  
**Course Type:** Theory

## Course Overview

Mathematics-I develops the mathematical foundations required for computing and information technology, including matrices, coordinate geometry, vectors, differential and integral calculus, and functions of several variables.

## Main Topics

- Matrices, determinants, adjoint and inverse
- Systems of linear equations and Cramer's rule
- Coordinate systems and conic sections
- Vectors, lines, planes and surfaces
- Differentiation and its applications
- Rolle's theorem and Mean Value Theorem
- Taylor's and Maclaurin's series
- Definite integration and applications
- Functions of several variables and partial derivatives
- Euler's theorem for homogeneous functions
